Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 29 October 2026

1. Data Retention and Scope

MindTheSpec is an academic solo research prototype. We store account credentials (using one-way salted bcrypt password hashes), project names, requirement definitions, and structured stakeholder interpretation submissions in a persistent relational database.

2. Blind Interpretation Confidentiality

Interpretation entries submitted during an active collecting session are restricted server-side. The database and API do not permit other project members, observers, or the project owner to retrieve another user's interpretation content prior to session lock.

3. Third-Party Disclosures & Trackers

MindTheSpec uses zero third-party analytics trackers, zero advertisement cookies, and zero hosted paid LLM APIs. Data is never sold, transmitted to third-party advertisers, or used for commercial training.

4. Session and Cookie Handling

We use a single cryptographically signed, HttpOnly session cookie (mindthespec_session) to maintain authentication state. No tracking tokens or sensitive requirement details are embedded in URL parameters or public logs.

5. Data Deletion Requests

Users participating in demonstration sessions may request removal of their demonstration accounts and project data by contacting the project administrator.
