Core Truth

Why requirements fail in quiet ways

Misunderstandings are rarely caused by bad engineers. They happen because everyone reads clear words with their own background assumptions.

01 / Subjective Terms

Vague Words Hide Disasters

Words like "responsive", "flexible", and "urgent" feel clear in meetings, but each team member envisions a completely different behavior.

02 / Social Pressure

The Loudest Voice Wins

In open calls, teammates nod along to the first confident speaker. Blind submission lets everyone speak their genuine technical view without influence.

03 / Prevention Cost

10x Cheaper Before Coding

Finding an assumption mismatch takes 10 minutes in MindTheSpec. Finding it during acceptance testing costs weeks of rewritten code and missed release dates.

Simple 3-Step Process

From confusion to clear consensus

No complex setup. Just three simple steps to keep your team aligned.

1

Write Blind Interpretations

Post a requirement. Developers, testers, and product managers write their expectations independently. No one sees anyone else's answers until the session closes.

2

Reveal Real Gaps

The system reveals all submitted viewpoints side by side. Concrete timeline and value mismatches are automatically highlighted for honest comparison.

3

Lock a Single Truth

The product owner records the official ruling and the exact reasons behind it. Everyone walks away with the same understanding and a permanent record.
